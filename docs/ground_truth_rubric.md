# Ground Truth Annotation Rubric

This rubric guides human annotation and is independent from the MDMS scoring formula.

## Holistic score

- 90–100: Exceptional fit.
- 75–89: Strong fit.
- 60–74: Moderate fit.
- 40–59: Weak fit.
- 0–39: Poor or non-relevant fit.

The 0–100 score is a holistic human suitability judgment. It must not be computed from MDMS component scores.

## Relevance grades

- 3: Highly Relevant
- 2: Relevant
- 1: Weak / Partially Relevant
- 0: Not Relevant

The configured evaluation threshold treats grades 2 and 3 as relevant.
